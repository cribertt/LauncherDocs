# Templates

Launcher Forge can provide different visual templates:

- **Prism Dock**
- **Alderon Classic**
- **Tactical Strike**
- **Nexus CyberPunk**

Template availability and customization depend on the active plan.

<Note>
  Templates do not necessarily support the same fields or visual sections.
</Note>
