# Team members

The **Team** page manages the developer accounts that can collaborate inside the active workspace.

Each member uses their own Launcher Forge account and security settings. Workspace resources are not shared through a common login.

<Info>
  The Team page represents membership in the active workspace. Games, launcher configuration, builds, players, subscription, add-ons, and usage remain scoped to that workspace.
</Info>

## Roles

Launcher Forge provides three workspace roles.

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control of the workspace, including subscription and add-on management. |
| **Admin** | Can manage workspace resources and operations, but cannot manage subscriptions or add-ons. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access. Can review information but cannot modify workspace data. |

### Owner

The Owner can:

- Rename the team or workspace.
- Invite and remove members.
- Change member roles.
- Manage games, launchers, branches, builds, players, and workspace settings.
- Manage subscriptions.
- Purchase, renew, or manage add-ons.
- Review plans, limits, and usage.

A workspace has one Owner.

### Admin

An Admin can perform operational management across the workspace, including:

- Manage games and their configuration.
- Manage launcher configuration.
- Create and manage branches.
- Publish and review builds.
- Manage players and access.
- Invite members and update operational roles when the interface permits.
- Review plan limits and usage.

An Admin cannot:

- Change or cancel the workspace subscription.
- Purchase, renew, or manage add-ons.

### Viewer

A Viewer has read-only access.

A Viewer can review:

- Team information.
- Games.
- Builds and versions.
- Branches.
- Player status.
- Plan and usage information available to the workspace.

A Viewer cannot create, edit, publish, delete, invite, revoke, ban, or change workspace configuration.

## Permission summary

```text
Owner
  → full workspace control
  → subscription and add-ons

Admin
  → operational workspace control
  → no subscription or add-on management

Viewer
  → read-only access
  → no modifications
```

## 1. Open Team

Select **Team** from the left sidebar.

The page shows:

- Team or workspace name.
- Number of active members.
- Number of pending invitations.
- Current members.
- Role assigned to each member.
- Invitation form.
- Pending invitations.

## 2. Rename the team

Select the pencil icon next to the team name.

Enter the new name and save it.

The team name identifies the workspace throughout the dashboard and workspace selector.

<Note>
  Renaming the team does not rename the games or launcher. Those resources keep their own names.
</Note>

## 3. Invite a member

In **Invite a member**:

1. Enter the collaborator's email address.
2. Select **Admin** or **Viewer**.
3. Select **Send invitation**.

The invitation link expires after **7 days**.

<Warning>
  Assign the minimum role required for the collaborator's responsibilities. Use Viewer when the person only needs to review project information.
</Warning>

## 4. Accept the invitation

The recipient receives an invitation by email.

They must:

1. Open the invitation link.
2. Sign in to Launcher Forge or create a developer account when required.
3. Accept the workspace invitation.

After acceptance:

- The account appears under active members.
- The assigned role becomes active.
- The workspace becomes available in the account's workspace selector.

Pending invitations remain visible until they are accepted, expire, or are cancelled.

## 5. Change a member role

Use the role selector beside an active member.

Available collaboration roles are:

- **Admin**
- **Viewer**

<Frame caption="Change an active member between Admin and Viewer">
  <img
    src="/images/screenshots/team-member-role-management.png"
    alt="Launcher Forge Team page with the Admin and Viewer role selector"
  />
</Frame>

Role changes affect the member's permissions in the active workspace.

They do not change:

- The member's account credentials.
- Their role in another workspace.
- Their access to unrelated workspaces.

## 6. Remove a member

Use the remove-member action beside the account.

Removing a member:

- Removes access to the current workspace.
- Does not delete the person's Launcher Forge account.
- Does not delete workspace resources created while they were a member.
- Does not remove access they may have in another workspace.

Review the account email and role before confirming removal.

## 7. Switch workspaces

An account can belong to more than one workspace by owning one or accepting invitations from other teams.

Open the workspace selector in the top navigation bar.

<Frame caption="Switch between the workspaces available to your account">
  <img
    src="/images/screenshots/workspace-switcher.png"
    alt="Launcher Forge workspace selector showing the active workspace and Team navigation"
  />
</Frame>

Selecting another workspace changes the active context for:

- Games.
- Launcher.
- Branches and builds.
- Players.
- Team members.
- Plan and subscription.
- Add-ons.
- Usage.

<Warning>
  Workspace data is isolated. Switching workspaces does not merge games, subscriptions, usage, or team permissions.
</Warning>

## Team-member limits

The number of members allowed in a workspace depends on the active plan.

When the member limit is reached, the workspace may need to:

- Remove an unused member.
- Upgrade the plan.
- Use an applicable workspace override or entitlement.

The current limit shown by the dashboard is the source of truth.

## Security recommendations

- Give Admin only to trusted collaborators.
- Use Viewer for reviewers, stakeholders, or read-only access.
- Remove members who no longer work on the project.
- Do not share one developer account between multiple people.
- Review pending invitations regularly.
- Verify the email address before sending an invitation.

<Check>
  Team access is configured correctly when every collaborator uses an individual account and has only the permissions required for their role.
</Check>
