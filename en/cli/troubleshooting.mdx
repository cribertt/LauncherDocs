# Common CLI errors

## Authentication error

Sign in again and confirm the expected account and workspace.

## Game or branch not found

Verify access, visibility, and the selected workspace.

## Storage limit reached

Review workspace usage and plan limits.

## Executable not found

Confirm the packaged folder and configured executable path.

## Network error or interrupted upload

Keep the terminal open, maintain a stable connection, and retry after confirming the previous upload is no longer active.
