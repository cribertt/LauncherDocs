# Create your first launcher

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Launchers">Open the **Launchers** section in the dashboard.</Step>
  <Step title="Create a launcher">Enter a clear name and unique slug.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">Select a template available to the workspace plan.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the language">Choose the launcher default language.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Create the initial record before adding detailed content.</Step>
</Steps>

## Initial checklist

- Name.
- Stable slug.
- Template.
- Default language.
- Icon.
- Status.
- Workspace.

<Warning>
  Avoid changing the slug after integrations, downloads, or public URLs depend on it.
</Warning>

Continue with [Configure the launcher](/en/launcher/configure-launcher).
