# Support tickets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a ticket">Select the category, priority, and provide a clear description.</Step>
  <Step title="Attach evidence">Add safe screenshots or logs without credentials.</Step>
  <Step title="Reply">Keep all related information in the same ticket.</Step>
  <Step title="Close">Close the ticket after the issue is resolved.</Step>
</Steps>
